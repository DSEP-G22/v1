# SOP: Cancellation and retention

## Symptoms
- Customer asks to cancel their subscription or close their account.
- Customer threatens to switch providers over price or repeated faults.

## Diagnosis
1. Read the ticket for the underlying reason (price, unresolved fault, moving house), routing
   a cancellation request straight to retention without the reason loses the chance to fix a
   still-open fault first.
2. If there is an open, unresolved fault ticket linked to the same customer, prioritise
   resolving the fault before discussing retention offers.

## Procedure
1. Acknowledge the cancellation request without immediately processing it.
2. If reason is price or a competing offer, note it for the retention team; do not promise a
   specific discount in the draft reply, retention offers require lead approval.
3. If reason is an unresolved fault, link this ticket to the open fault ticket and prioritise
   that first.

## Compliance note
Do not state or imply the account is already cancelled until an `agent_decision` confirming the
cancellation has been recorded.
